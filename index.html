Getting startedWhat should I check before asking a question?

Refer to the slides, video recording and FITS user manual in Microsoft Teams before contacting HEJIM.

Always include your full name, matric number and internship course code when asking a question or making a request.

Contact officers or Dr. Lam Meng Chun during working hours. Please avoid contacting them after working hours or during holidays.

RequestsHow long does it take to process my request?

FITS requests and enquiries to HEJIM require 3–5 working days. It includes adding company, request cover letter, enquiry that email to HEJIM. Follow up on the third working day, rather than on the same day.

Include your full name, matric number and internship course code.

Cover letterCan I change the dates in my internship cover letter?

The second page of the cover letter contains a response form for you and the company to agree on your internship start and end dates. FTSM recognises 20 weeks of internship.

Update the actual dates in FITS when reporting for duty. You do not need to email HEJIM to change the dates unless the company requests a revised letter or your proposed start date is after the applicable deadline.

If necessary, email HEJIM to request an adjustment. The end date should be set to the start date plus 20 weeks.

Do not edit any internship cover letter or document issued by UKM yourself.

DurationCan my internship last longer than 20 weeks?

FTSM can only issue an internship cover letter for a maximum of 20 weeks.

Any extension beyond 20 weeks is subject to mutual agreement between you and the company, at your own risk and responsibility. You are advised to secure your own insurance coverage for the extension.

The company must agree to evaluate you in week 20.

FITS accountWhen will my industry supervisor receive an activation email?

FITS automatically sends an activation email when you confirm your industry supervisor’s email address during reporting for duty.

The email subject is “Activation for FITs Account – FTSM Industrial Training System, UKM”.

FITS accountWhat if my supervisor cannot find the activation email?
  • Check that you entered the correct supervisor email address in FITS.
  • Ask your supervisor to check their inbox, junk and spam folders.
  • Your supervisor may already have an account from a previous internship.

If your supervisor can log in, no activation is needed. If they have forgotten their password, they can use the forgot password function.

DeadlinesWhat is the latest date I can start my internship?
  • TTTT411A: 5 October 2026.
  • TTTT4056: 12 October 2026.

An internship that starts after the applicable deadline is not valid. Contact HEJIM if your proposed start date is after the deadline.

Validity & disciplineWhat can cause an internship to fail or become invalid?

The FAQ lists the following circumstances:

  • Changing your internship placement without permission from HEJIM.
  • Receiving two warning letters.
  • Disciplinary issues.
  • Starting your internship after the applicable deadline.
  • Did not pass the Final Year Project

Contact HEJIM for advice on your individual circumstances.

PlacementCan I change my internship company?

You must obtain permission from HEJIM before changing your internship placement. Contact HEJIM before quitting your current company.

Changing your placement without HEJIM’s permission can cause your internship to fail or become invalid.

Medical & personal leaveWhat if I take more than five days of medical or personal leave?

If you take more than five days of medical or personal leave, you must make up for the period of leave taken.

For hospitalisation leave (usually exceeding seven consecutive days), contact HEJIM for further clarification.
